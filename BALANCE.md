## Headline

In short: the skeletons are too easy, fights against them are over too fast. Reasonable player boards clear round 1 **100%** of the time (target ~95%) and beat the round 5 boss **100%** (target ~33%). Head-to-head at equal gold the strongest unit is the **Barbarian (54.3%)** and the weakest the **Rogue (45.5%)** (target 40%-60%). Fights against the skeletons average **8.2 s** (target 10-25 s) with 0 timeouts.
