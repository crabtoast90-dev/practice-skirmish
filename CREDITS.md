Every third-party asset, with source and license. Source files for the KayKit packs are committed in `gltf/`; only what the game uses is moved into `public/models/`.

- three.js (MIT): https://threejs.org
- Vite (MIT, build tool): https://vite.dev
- Knight, Barbarian, Rogue, Mage and Ranger models, the shared Rig_Medium animation files (`Rig_Medium_General.glb`, `Rig_Medium_MovementBasic.glb`, `Rig_Medium_CombatMelee.glb`, `Rig_Medium_CombatRanged.glb`, `Rig_Medium_Special.glb`) in `public/models/units/`, and the heroes' weapons (sword, badge shield, two-handed axe, dagger, staff, wand, bow, with their textures) in `public/models/units/weapons/`: KayKit Adventurers Character Pack 2.0 by Kay Lousberg (CC0), https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers, license https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Skeleton Warrior, Skeleton Rogue, Skeleton Mage and Skeleton Minion models in `public/models/units/`, and the skeletons' blade, axe and large shield (with `skeleton_texture.png`) in `public/models/units/weapons/`: KayKit Skeletons Character Pack by Kay Lousberg (CC0), https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-skeletons, license https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Mug of ale (`mug_full.gltf`, `.bin`, `barbarian_texture.png`) in `public/models/props/`: KayKit Adventurers Character Pack 2.0 accessories (CC0), same source and license as above.
- Table and board wood, inlaid grid, bevelled board frame, desk lamp, painted mini bases, HP bars, combat effects (projectiles, special glows and bursts, hit flashes, damage numbers) and the interface: built procedurally in code (no third-party source).
- Painted look (`?look=painted` only, an experiment): `public/look/painted-laptop.png` and `painted-phone.png`, and the variants in `look-experiment/`, are paint-overs of our own renders of the board, made with Meshy's image-to-image API (Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image 2 models) on the user's Meshy account. No third-party art went in.
